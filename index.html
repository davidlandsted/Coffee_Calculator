Brewing Guide

  1. Grind coffee to a coarse consistency (like sea salt).
  2. Add the ground coffee to your French press.
  3. Pour hot water (90–96 °C / 194–205 °F) over the grounds.
  4. Stir gently and fit the lid on (plunger up).
  5. Steep for 4 minutes — use the timer above!
  6. Press the plunger down slowly and steadily.
  7. Pour immediately and enjoy.
